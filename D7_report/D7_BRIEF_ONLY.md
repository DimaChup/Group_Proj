## Brief Page 9 — D7 Specification (FULL verbatim)

### D7 Individual Reflective Report (50%)

> - Submission: Individual submission of single PDF.
> - Maximum length: 5 pages, excluding cover page and appendices.

> Answer the questions below, reflecting on <ins>your specific experiences</ins>, within <ins>your specific team</ins>. Your report will only be reviewed by the unit marking team and appropriate exam boards/assessors, so you are free to discuss specifics.

> A useful framework to encourage *constructive* reflection is to consider 'what went well', and 'even better if'. You may wish to refer to concepts detailed in the Professional Practice slides and videos.

> You may include anecdotes of team activities, but the focus of this reflection is on *what you did* to influence the team's working practices and outputs, *how you developed*, and *what you might do differently* in comparable future situations.

#### 1. My role
> a. What were your roles and responsibilities in this project? Which of these were explicitly allocated, and which did you take on/were you given implicitly? Did they change over the course of the project?
> b. What elements of your contribution are you most happy about and/or proud of?
> c. What different things might you focus on in your future work?

#### 2. My team
> a. How was your team structured? Was this discussed explicitly? What changed over the course of the project?
> b. What elements of team working do you think were most impactful on your outputs?
> c. What would you change if you were to do a similar project in future?

#### 3. My impact
> a. How did your contributions drive the team forward and contribute to success?
> b. What might you add or re-focus on in future projects?

#### 4. My support
> a. What did other team members do that helped you work more effectively?
> b. What actions did you take that helped other individuals and/or the team as a whole work more effectively?

### Relevant AHEP4 standards (verbatim)

> **M16.** Function effectively as an individual, and as a member or leader of a team. Evaluate effectiveness of own and team performance.

> **M17.** Communicate effectively on complex engineering matters with technical and non-technical audiences, evaluating the effectiveness of the methods used.

## Key Observations from the Verbatim Text

These are subtle requirements that are easy to miss on a first read — **no interpretation, just pointing at what the brief literally says**:

1. **"5 pages, excluding cover page and appendices"** — the cover page and appendices do **not** count toward the 5-page limit. Appendices are explicitly allowed.
2. **"Your report will only be reviewed by the unit marking team and appropriate exam boards/assessors, so you are <ins>free to discuss specifics</ins>"** — the brief explicitly permits naming individuals, describing conflicts, and discussing confidential team details.
3. **"reflecting on <ins>your specific experiences</ins>, within <ins>your specific team</ins>"** — emphasis on *specific*, not generic. Generic reflection is off-brief.
4. **"'what went well', and 'even better if'"** — the brief explicitly names this constructive framework. Using these phrases / this structure is aligned with the brief.
5. **"the focus of this reflection is on *what you did* to influence the team's working practices and outputs, *how you developed*, and *what you might do differently*"** — three focus areas, all first-person and action-oriented. Describing what the team did without your influence is off-brief.
6. **"You may include anecdotes of team activities"** — anecdotes are permitted but not the focus.
7. **"You may wish to refer to concepts detailed in the Professional Practice slides and videos"** — referencing taught Professional Practice content is encouraged.
8. **Three criteria only in the rubric**: Teamwork, Self-management, Insight. **No separate "communication" or "technical" criterion** — despite M17 being about communication, the rubric does not have a communication row. Communication quality is folded into overall presentation/level-7 standards.
9. **AHEP M16 = individual + team function + evaluate effectiveness** (maps to Teamwork + Self-management). **AHEP M17 = communicate effectively on complex engineering matters to technical and non-technical audiences, evaluating effectiveness of methods used** (maps to how the reflection itself is written).
10. **AI policy for D7 is Category 2: Minimal** (Reporting and presentation) — stricter than software development's Category 3: Selective.
11. **Release 2.2 (6 Apr 2026) added "individual report detail"** — the current detailed D7 spec on page 9 is a late addition; earlier drafts had less detail.
12. **Rubric top band explicitly rewards "provide effective feedback to others to aid their self-development"** — i.e. the reflection should show you helped teammates develop, not just yourself.
13. **Rubric top band for Teamwork rewards "creativity and flexibility that is responsive to group members' interests"** — not just meeting obligations.
14. **"Manage conflict"** appears in both the 62-68 and 72-78 and 83-100 Teamwork bands — conflict management is an explicit scoring lever from 62 upward.
15. **Mark-band gaps are non-uniform**: the top band jumps `83, 94, 100` — there is no `88` or `90` anchor. Marks above 78 require outstanding evidence.
