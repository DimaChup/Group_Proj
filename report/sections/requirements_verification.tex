Of the 12 requirements, 8 are fully verified at bench or higher fidelity (R01--R04, R09--R12), 3 are verified in simulation only (R05--R07), and 1 has a partial gap (R06 not field-tested with a live PLB). Recent code improvements closed several evidence gaps: R01 now includes a runtime Flight Area geofence with emergency RTL (not just plan-time clipping), R03 checks take-off location distance at arming, R04 enforces a 50\,m hard cap in the navigation module, R07 prints the actual casualty distance at landing, R10 saves structured detection images and JSON metadata to \texttt{mission\_detections/}, and R12 has an explicit MIT \texttt{LICENSE} file. The highest-risk requirements---R01 (flight area), R02 (SSSI), and R09 (override)---are each protected by multiple independent mechanisms verified at both the simulation and hardware tiers. The most significant verification gap is the absence of outdoor flight data for R05 (search and detection) and R07 (landing accuracy); both are verified quantitatively in simulation and through DJI video proxy analysis but remain unvalidated under real flight conditions due to weather cancellation. Full evidence narratives for each requirement are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:req-detail}.
