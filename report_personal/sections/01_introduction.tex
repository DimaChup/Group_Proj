\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

I was the software and computer vision lead for an autonomous SAR drone (AENGM0074 Group Design Project), responsible for the full software stack: state machine, vision system, search planning, target localisation, ground station, and testing infrastructure (${\sim}$15,000 lines of Python). This report examines my contributions through AHEP4 learning outcomes~\cite{ahep4}: design and problem-solving (M5), societal and environmental impact (M7), and teamwork and communication (M16, M17).
