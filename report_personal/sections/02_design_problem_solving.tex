\section{Design and Problem-Solving (M5)}
\label{sec:design}

\subsection{Original Solutions to Complex Problems}

The core challenge was building a fully autonomous mission on a Raspberry Pi~5~\cite{rpi5} with no GPU, operating at under 5~FPS, where software failures have physical consequences.

\textbf{Dual-backend vision architecture.} The vision system exposes a single interface---\texttt{detect\_in\_image(frame)}---while auto-detecting the platform and selecting the appropriate backend (Ultralytics on laptop, TFLite~\cite{tflite} on Pi). This abstraction let me add lens undistortion and model swapping without changing any other module.

\textbf{Simulation-first development.} Following Sch{\"o}n's reflection-in-action principle~\cite{schon1983}, I built an interactive simulator with configurable GPS drift, camera shake, and inference throttling, proving every algorithm under pessimistic conditions before deployment.

\textbf{Target localisation under uncertainty.} Converting pixel detections to GPS coordinates required fusing camera geometry with drone telemetry via inverse-variance weighting ($w = 1/h^2$), with a bonus for near-centre detections where projection error is minimised. Video analysis revealed GPS timing lag (100--200\,ms), causing ${\sim}$1\,m directional error at 5\,m/s, though statistical averaging proved sufficient.

\subsection{Safety-Critical Design}

Working on a system that controls a physical aircraft shaped every design decision~\cite{leveson2011}. I designed a five-step progressive testing sequence---from Mission Planner waypoints (no custom code) through to full autonomy---following aerospace verification practices~\cite{koopman2019safe}. Each step adds exactly one variable, making failure causes unambiguous. Additional measures included automatic geofencing, RC kill switch priority, and headless-first field operation.

\subsection{STEEPLE Considerations}

Safety was addressed through progressive testing and geofencing; environmental impact through SSSI no-fly zone protection and choosing the 5\,W Pi over GPU alternatives. The \pounds60 platform makes SAR accessible to resource-limited organisations, legal compliance followed CAA regulations, and the ethical dimension of autonomous decision-making was handled by keeping a human in the verification loop.
